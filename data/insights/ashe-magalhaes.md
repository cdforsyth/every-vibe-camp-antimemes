## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Integrations Add Noise, Not Signal

**INSIGHT:** The people who matter in your network are the ones you're naturally thinking about and adding notes on - not the ones who show up in automated email syncs or LinkedIn imports.

**CATEGORY:** Antimeme

**CLASSIFICATION:** Diagnostic

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Ignoring
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Vertigo

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 4
- Specificity: 5
- Surprise: 5
- Shareability: 5

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Psychological / Implementation

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** "More data is better" is so deeply ingrained that removing integrations feels like going backwards
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Every CRM and contact tool markets itself on integrations - the industry incentive is to add more, not less

**Why it resists spreading:**
- The entire contact management industry is built on the opposite premise
- It feels irresponsible to "not have complete data"
- The work of manually noting what matters feels slower than automated sync
- You'd have to admit that most of your "contacts" don't actually matter to you

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The illusion of a comprehensive network
- The safety blanket of "I have everyone's info"
- The convenience of automatic syncing

**Verification:**
> "Integrations can add unnecessary noise to a rolodex and actually the people that are top of mind or that I care about are people that I'm naturally thinking about, meeting with live, adding notes on."

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Implementation

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 8

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Requires upfront architecture work that most people skip
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** The compounding benefit is invisible until you've been doing it for months

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Setting up unified context requires significant upfront investment
- Each individual project seems to work fine without it
- The benefit only becomes apparent over time (delayed gratification)
- Most AI tools don't support this kind of context federation

- The speed of starting fresh without architectural overhead

**Pattern Across Insights:**
Ashe's insights cluster around intentionality and quality over quantity. Her approach to AI is the opposite of "integrate everything" - it's curate carefully, compound over time, and protect the conditions for creative work. Where most people add more (more integrations, more context, more agents), she's selective about what actually matters. The Rolodex-as-journal insight is particularly powerful because it reframes a productivity tool as a reflective practice. The common thread: meaningful signal requires actively filtering out noise, even when the noise feels like "data."
