## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. It's More About the Vibe Than the Coding

**INSIGHT:** The key to vibe coding success isn't technical skill - it's having fun and being genuinely motivated by what you're building. The "vibe" part matters more than the "coding" part.

**CATEGORY:** Antimeme

**CLASSIFICATION:** Diagnostic

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Ignoring
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Recognition

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 4
- Specificity: 4
- Surprise: 4
- Shareability: 5

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Psychological / Social

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 8

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Psychological Antimeme:** People approach new tools with "I need to learn this" energy rather than "I want to play with this" energy
- **Social Antimeme:** "Having fun" sounds unserious; productivity culture demands optimization mindset

**Why it resists spreading:**
- "Vibe coding" already has the word "vibe" but people focus on "coding"
- Fun feels like a side effect, not the strategy
- Technical communities value skill over enjoyment
- The productivity mindset prioritizes output over experience

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The illusion that mastery comes before enjoyment
- The seriousness that makes you feel like a "real" developer

**Verification:**
> "It's actually more about the vibe than the coding. It's like, if you are having fun building what you want to build, that's actually the most important motivation."

The common thread: take building less seriously as work and more seriously as play.
